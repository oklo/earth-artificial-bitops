# Assumptions

- Temperature for the headline Landauer yardstick is `T = 300 K`.
- Inclusive artificial computation includes SHA-256 proof-of-work.
- The non-PoW series is diagnostic and should not be treated as the talk-facing all-computation headline.
- SHA-256 proof-of-work is counted as computation for this exercise even though its economic function differs from scientific, business, AI, or consumer computation.
- Historical values are order-of-magnitude reconstructions with deliberately broad uncertainty intervals.
- The 2025 row is reconstructed/estimated from model/current data because the talk date is in 2026.
- The 2030 and 2035 rows are parsimonious log-space extrapolations of aggregate bitops history.  They are not allocated to a detailed technology stack.
- Future threshold calculations ask what happens if reversible computing is not economically or technically available at scale.
