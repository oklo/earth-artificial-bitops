# Sources and Anchors

This file records source anchors used by the model and talk.  Some numerical values are inherited from the prior analysis and should be rechecked before paper-grade publication.  The links below were spot-checked during repository preparation on 2026-07-25.

- NIST/CODATA Boltzmann constant: `k_B = 1.380649e-23 J/K`.
  URL: https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/cuu/Value?k

- Landauer bound: irreversible erasure of one bit has thermodynamic lower bound `k_B T ln 2`.
  Anchor: R. Landauer, "Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process", IBM Journal of Research and Development, 1961.
  DOI URL: https://doi.org/10.1147/rd.53.0183

- IEA global energy and data-centre electricity anchors.
  Current primary energy used here is about `650 EJ/year` in the 2024/2025 neighborhood.  Data-centre electricity anchors used in the talk include about `415 TWh` in 2024 and about `945 TWh` by 2030.
  URL: https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai

- NASA total solar irradiance anchor: approximately `1361.6 W/m^2`.
  URL: https://earth.gsfc.nasa.gov/climate/projects/solar-irradiance/science

- Earth-intercepted solar power: `S pi R_earth^2`, approximately `1.74e17 W` for `S ~ 1361.6 W/m^2`.
  Source noted in prior analysis; verify Earth-radius convention before publication.

- IAU nominal solar luminosity: `L_sun = 3.828e26 W`.
  URL: https://www.iau.org/static/resolutions/IAU2015_English.pdf

- Bitcoin/SHA-256 hashrate anchor: around `1 ZH/s` in the 2025/2026 neighborhood.
  URL: https://www.blockchain.com/explorer/charts/hash-rate

- Ethereum Merge: September 15, 2022; Ethereum moved from proof-of-work to proof-of-stake and its energy use dropped sharply.
  URL: https://ethereum.org/en/energy-consumption/

- Hilbert and Lopez global technological capacity: useful anchor for historical general-purpose computation.
  Citation anchor: Martin Hilbert and Priscila Lopez, "The World's Technological Capacity to Store, Communicate, and Compute Information", Science, 2011.
  DOI URL: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1200970

- Koomey/Masanet data-centre electricity estimates: useful anchors for data-centre electricity around 2000 through 2018.
  Citation anchor: Eric Masanet et al., "Recalibrating global data center energy-use estimates", Science, 2020.
  DOI URL: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aba3758

- Ericsson/ITU/GSMA network and mobile anchors: useful for telecom electricity and installed-base context.
  Source noted in prior analysis; verify exact report/year before publication.

- ENIAC, Colossus, IBM 1401 early-computing anchors.
  Source noted in prior analysis; verify exact machine-performance and fleet-count assumptions before publication.
