# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (2026-11)

Results from 0.1.0 are superseded. An adversarial review
(`docs/review_2026-11-11.md`) found that several headline numbers were
hand-entered rather than computed. Everything in the README, `data/processed/`
and `figures/` is now generated by `make all` and checked by
`scripts/check_outputs.py`.

### Numbers that changed

| Quantity | 0.1.0 | 0.2.0 | Why |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Bitops/s, 2025 | 7.9e25 | 9.8e25 | Uses the 2025 hashrate instead of a copy of 2026 |
| Bitops/s, 2030 | 1.89e27 | 1.6e26 | Was a literal; now a component projection (p50) |
| Bitops/s, 2035 | 4.51e28 | 4.2e26 (p10-p90 1.2e26 to 1.6e27) | Same |
| Year computation reaches world primary energy | 2065 or 2090 | 2059-2070 | Were hard-coded anchors; now fitted trends with an efficiency ceiling |
| Year computation reaches Earth-intercepted sunlight | 2089 or 2170 | 2077-2092 | Same |
| Year computation reaches solar luminosity | 2142 or 2473 | 2122-2136 | Same |
| Maximum Landauer efficiency of irreversible logic | 0.25-1.0 | 1e-3 to 1e-2 (1.0 shown as a bound) | Reliable switching needs about 35-60 kT per operation |

Do not cite the 0.1.0 values for 2030, 2035 or any crossing year.

### Added

- `projection_model.py`: 2026-2035 Monte Carlo with separate proof-of-work, AI and other components.
- Fitted long-run trends (1945-2026 and 2000-2026), efficiency ceilings, a reversible-computing branch and demand sensitivities in `future_model.py`.
- 1945 and 2025 anchors; tables from 1945 onward including bitops/erg and annual energy.
- Category-resolved stacked figures, restored from archived shares (`data/reference/`).
- A climate threshold (1% of absorbed sunlight) and a growing-primary-energy threshold.
- Consistency checks across all generated files.

### Removed

- Hand-entered 2030/2035 table rows and hard-coded crossing years.
- `earth_artificial_bitops_future_projection_summary.csv`, `..._future_timeseries_central.csv`, `..._future_timeseries_constant_50pct.csv` (replaced by `..._future_threshold_crossings.csv`, `..._future_trend_fits.csv`, `..._future_timeseries.csv`).

### Known limitations

See "Caveats" in the README. The main one: the 1945-2020 medians are still hand-set anchors.
